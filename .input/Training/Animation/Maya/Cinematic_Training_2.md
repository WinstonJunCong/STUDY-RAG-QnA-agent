# Cinematic Training 2

**LEVEL 2 \- Dialogue**

## **🔊 Let’s Talk**

* This training is more expression based  
  * Explore the full facial controls on a rig  
  * Taking into consideration on how expressions affect the muscle in our face  
* The trainer can either prepare the audio template or the trainee can do it instead but will need approval from the trainer for suitability  
* **You are required to use one character only**  
* The camera setup should be simple   
  * can be a **half body shot** (full body shot is unnecessary)  
  * focus is on lip sync / facial expression  
* The **audio must be at least one sentence and not just a single syllable**  
* The duration should be **no more than 10 seconds**  
* **Please ensure there is some form of secondary action but keep in mind that the main focus is geared towards lip sync & facial expression**
