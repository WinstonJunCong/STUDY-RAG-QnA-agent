# Cinematic Training 3

**LEVEL 3 \- Lights, Camera\!**

## **🎬 ...and Action\!**

* Apply what you have learned from Level 1 and 2  
* The duration should be within **5 \~ 10 seconds**  
* There should be some form of interaction between 2 characters and/or with a prop   
  * The interaction with the prop should be complex (..and not just picking it up and putting it down…)  
* Please ensure that the camera setup is complex, dynamic and that the secondary actions are animated completely
