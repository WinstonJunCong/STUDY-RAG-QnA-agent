**LEVEL 2 - Advance Body Mechanic**

## **🤜🏻 Attack**

- Please create a simple punch or kick motion that is moving progressively forward

- **The world (global) control should be moving**

- This motion should start and end from the Idle pose

- For training purposes, please create a target for you to hit (It can be as simple as a cube)

- Please ensure that your body mechanics look believable

- The duration of this motion is 3 MDs

## **🔥 Skill**

- Please create a motion that reflects the character performing a skill that can damage or debuff an enemy by magic or by buffing oneself

- **Your character can be moving progressively forward or performing on the spot**

- **The world (global) control will depend on whether you choose to move forward or stay on the same spot**

- Please ensure that your body mechanics look believable

- You may use proxy effects if necessary

- For training purposes, please create a target for you to hit (It can be as simple as a cube)

- This motion should start and end from the Idle pose

- The duration of this motion is 2.5 MDs

## **💥 Combo Attack**

- Please create a 2 combo attack motion continuing from your initial attack motion followed by another attack motion of your choice that shows your character moving progressively forward

- **The second attack motion should immediately start at the first hit frame or 3 \~ 4 frames after the 1st hit frame**

- **The world (global) control should be moving**

- This motion should start and end from the Idle pose

- For training purposes, please create a target for you to hit (It can be as simple as a cube)

- The duration of this motion is 4 MDs

## **💀 Death**

- Please create a motion that shows your character dying

- This motion will start from the Idle pose and ends in a dying pose of your choice

- This motion should be around 120 frames

- The duration of this motion is 3 MDs

## **👼🏻 Revive**

- Please create a motion that shows your character being revived

- This motion will start from the Death pose and end in the Idle pose

- This motion should be around 120 frames

- The duration of this motion is 3 MDs
