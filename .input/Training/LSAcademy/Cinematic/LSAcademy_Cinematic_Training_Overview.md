# Cinematic Training Overview

## **🥅 Goal**

Cinematic

- To learn how to breathe life and personality to a virtual character

- Staging your acting to support the overall story and character arc

- Understanding on how to reveal a character's internal thought process through external acting

- Improving on lip sync to make the acting more believable

# **🎬 Style**

- Snappy/Cartoony/Emotional/Lively acting for cinematics

# **[📁] Folder Structure**

Generally, the folders can be divided into 2 directories, they are;

- LADY_PROJ

- WIP

### **⭐ LADY_PROJ** 

- This directory is considered the **Master Directory**

- Once the motion is considered approved, both maya and playblast files will be safely stored under their respective type of training for each individual artist for future references or as material for Lemon Sky's Animation Reel

> **L:\\LADY\\WORKFILES\\LADY_PROJ\\01_CourseMaterials\\\[YY_MM\]\_BatchXX\\\[Cinematic\]\\\[ArtistName\]\\\[MotionName\]**
>
> ![](../../../Images/LSAcademy_Cinematic_Training_Overview/media/image1.png)

### **👷🏻‍♀️ WIP** 

- This directory is where all Work-In-Progress (WIP) files will go to under their respective artist name

- Each respective motion folder should always have a Previews and WIP folder to store your working maya and playblast files

- You may put the finalized and cleaned up files outside the folders so that it may be copied to the Master Directory

#### **Playblast files**

> **L:\\LADY\\WORKFILES\\LADY_PROJ\\01_CourseMaterials\\\[YY_MM\]\_BatchXX\\\[Cinematic\]\\\[ArtistName\]\\\[MotionName\]\\\[Previews\]**

#### **WIP Maya files**

> **L:\\LADY\\WORKFILES\\LADY_PROJ\\01_CourseMaterials\\\[YY_MM\]\_BatchXX\\\[Cinematic\]\\\[ArtistName\]\\\[MotionName\]\\\[WIP\]**

![](../../../Images/LSAcademy_Cinematic_Training_Overview/media/image2.png)
